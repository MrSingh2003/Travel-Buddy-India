### Project Introduction: Travel Buddy India

"My project is **Travel Buddy India**, a smart travel decision tool designed to simplify and enhance the experience of traveling within India.

The core problem it solves is the fragmented and often untrustworthy nature of travel planning. Travelers struggle to find transparent pricing, reliable local transport, and truly personalized recommendations that go beyond the usual tourist traps.

Instead of just being another travel information portal, Travel Buddy India acts as an AI-powered assistant. The standout feature is the **AI Trip Planner**. A user can input their destination, budget, dates, and interests, and the AI generates a complete itinerary with a unique **suitability score**. This score, along with a detailed budget breakdown and weather-aware packing advice, gives the user data-driven confidence in their travel decisions.

Other key features include:
*   A **Smart Place Finder** to discover locations that match a user's unique style.
*   A **Local Travel Navigator** to find and book verified local cabs, buses, and trains, which is a major challenge in many parts of India.
*   A comprehensive **Accommodations** search that includes both hotels and budget-friendly dharamshalas.

The application is built on a modern tech stack: React with Vite, React Router, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS with ShadCN for the UI. For the backend, it uses Firebase for authentication, supporting email, Google, and phone OTP login. The AI capabilities are powered by Google's Genkit and Gemini models.

What makes Travel Buddy India unique is its relentless focus on **decision support**. It's not just about *what* you can do, but *why* a particular plan is a good fit for *you*, giving travelers the confidence to explore India more freely and intelligently."
